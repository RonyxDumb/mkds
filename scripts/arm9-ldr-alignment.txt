ARM word-load analysis uses the alternative aligned-read and rotate-right implementation already commented in Ghidra 11.3.2 ARMinstructions.sinc. Applied to ARM5t_le.sla only, assuming legacy little-endian ARMv5 loads with alignment checking disabled. No ROM byte changes. Thumb and ARM7 instruction models are unchanged.
# The following form of ldr assumes alignment checking is on
:ldr^COND Rd,addrmode2 		is $(AMODE) & ARMcond=1 & COND & c2627=1 & B22=0 & L20=1 & Rd & (I25=0 | (I25=1 & c0404=0)) & addrmode2
{
  build COND;
  build addrmode2;
  tmp:4=addrmode2&0xfffffffc;
  tmp2:4=(addrmode2&3)<<3;
  Rd=*tmp;
  Rd = (Rd >> tmp2) | (Rd << (32-tmp2));
}
